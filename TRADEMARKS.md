# Graphden Trademark Policy

"Graphden", the Graphden logo, and the `graphden.dev` domain are trademarks
of Artem Markov (the "Marks"), whether or not registered in a given
jurisdiction. The software license ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) covers the code;
it does **not** grant any right to the Marks. This policy does.

The goal is simple: you can build on, extend, package for, and talk about
Graphden freely — but nobody should be confused about what is the Graphden
project and what is somebody else's product.

## You may, without asking

- Refer to the software by name to describe what you do: "built on
  Graphden", "compatible with Graphden", "a Graphden package", "runs on a
  self-hosted Graphden instance".
- Name a package, integration, or tool for Graphden `graphden-<name>` or
  "`<name>` for Graphden", provided it does not claim to be official and
  its own name is the distinctive part.
- Redistribute **unmodified** Graphden under the AGPL-3.0 using the name
  and logo as shipped.
- Write articles, tutorials, talks, and reviews that use the name and
  logo to identify the project.
- Fork the repository on GitHub. A fork that keeps the upstream name is
  fine as long as it is presented as a fork of Graphden and not as
  Graphden.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a modified version, a fork you distribute or host as your own
  product, a hosted service, a company, a product line, or a domain
  "Graphden" or anything confusingly similar (for example "Graphden
  Cloud", "GraphdenHub", `graphden.<tld>`). Modified versions distributed
  under a different name may say "based on Graphden".
- Use the logo as your own logo, or in a way that suggests your product or
  service is provided, endorsed, or supported by the Graphden project.
- Register the Marks, or names confusingly similar to them, as trademarks,
  company names, app-store names, social-media handles, or domains.
- Sell merchandise bearing the Marks.

## Official channels

- Website and hosted service: https://graphden.dev, https://app.graphden.dev
- Source: https://github.com/Graphden

Anything else claiming to be "official Graphden" is not.

## Questions and permission requests

Write to legal@graphden.dev. Requests that fit the spirit of this policy
are usually granted, and a reply that a use is fine is all the permission
you need.

This policy may be updated; the version in the repository's default branch
is the current one.
