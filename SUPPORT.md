# Support

Two lanes: community support is free and public; paid support buys a
response time and a person who already knows your deployment.

## Community (free)

- **Questions and how-to** — [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Graphden/graphden/discussions)
  or [Discord](https://discord.gg/ej2unfzKXq). Read
  [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) and the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md) first; most
  first questions are answered there.
- **Bugs** — [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Graphden/graphden/issues)
  with the output of `GET /version`, the steps, and what you expected.
- **Security** — never a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Answer time** — best effort, usually within a few days. There is one
  maintainer.

## Paid support (self-host)

For organisations running Graphden on their own infrastructure:

| | Included |
|---|---|
| Response | next business day, by email or a private channel |
| Scope | upgrades and migrations, incident diagnosis, configuration review, a monthly check-in |
| Time | up to 4 hours per month; extra hours at the published hourly rate |
| Diagnosis material | `bb support-bundle` — versions, configuration with secrets stripped, recent errors |

Pricing is on [graphden.dev](https://graphden.dev/#pricing). Ask at
`support@graphden.dev` with your `GET /version` output and a sentence on
what you run.

## Implementation work

Integrations, automations and internal services built on Graphden for you,
as a fixed-price pilot of two to four weeks followed by monthly support.
Details on [graphden.dev](https://graphden.dev/#services); write to
`hello@graphden.dev`.

## What paid support does not change

The core stays AGPL-3.0 for everyone; paid customers get time and
attention, never a different codebase. Features requested under support
land in the public repository like any other change.
