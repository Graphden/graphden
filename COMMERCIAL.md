## Who needs one

Graphden's core is licensed under the
[GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). That is fine for most users: running Graphden,
building on it, self-hosting it, and loading your own packages into it
do not require you to publish anything — see the
[Package and Embedding Exception](LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md) for the exact
boundary.

A commercial licence is for organisations that

- modify the core itself and cannot publish those modifications;
- embed the core in a product or service offered to third parties and
  cannot accept AGPL section 13 (network use is distribution); or
- have a policy that forbids AGPL dependencies outright.

If none of these apply, you do not need one.

## What it grants

- The right to use, modify and run the core under the agreed terms,
  without the AGPL's source-disclosure obligations.
- Per organisation, for a term of one year, renewable.
- Updates released during the term.
- No right to redistribute the core as such, and no right to the
  "Graphden" name beyond [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

## What it does not include

- The multi-tenant cloud layer and the cloud control plane: separate
  proprietary works, not part of this repository and not licensed here.
- Support or implementation work — those are separate, see
  [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

## Why this is possible

Contributions are accepted under a [CLA](CLA.md) that assigns rights to
the project owner. That is what lets one owner offer the same code under a
second licence. The AGPL grant is never withdrawn: every commercial
licensee's version also exists in the public repository.

## Price and contact

Priced per organisation and year; ask at `licensing@graphden.dev` with a
sentence on how you intend to use Graphden. Indicative pricing is on
[graphden.dev](https://graphden.dev/#pricing).
