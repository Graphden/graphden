# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Graphden — filing issues, opening pull requests,
discussing designs — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the project:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions — and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@graphden.dev**. Reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate.
